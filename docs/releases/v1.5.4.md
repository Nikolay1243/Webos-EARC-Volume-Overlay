## Compatibility check

- The setup screen has a new **Check compatibility** button. Press it, then press volume up and down on the TV remote during the 10-second countdown.
- The check reads what the soundbar sent back in the TV's HDMI-CEC log and gives a plain verdict:
  - **Compatible:** the soundbar reports its volume after each press; the overlay shows the exact level.
  - **Mostly compatible:** it answers some presses; the overlay works but may skip a step.
  - **Compatible (slower mode):** it doesn't report directly, but the TV's stored value follows it.
  - **Not compatible:** it accepts the TV's volume commands but never reports its level (for example a Samsung HW-Q990B on an LG C2). The app can be uninstalled.
  - Hints when no presses were seen, when sound isn't going to a soundbar, or when the TV keeps no CEC log.
- The check only reads; nothing is sent to the soundbar.
