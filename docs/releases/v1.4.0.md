## One app for settings and volume display

- Merges the settings menu and volume display into **one app and one IPK**. Open **eARC Volume Overlay** from the launcher for Enable, Disable, and Test overlay.
- Enable starts the independent background watcher and preserves the startup hook across hard reboots. Closing or backing out of settings leaves the enabled watcher running; Disable also removes its automatic startup hook.
- Uses a passive native floating window for the volume display, keeping pointer clicks, arrow keys, and OK available to the app underneath. Settings remains a normal interactive card.
- Fixes settings remaining hidden in a mode that prevented subsequent volume displays until reboot.
- Adds **eArc Volume** text to the app icon, with fresh image filenames so the launcher refreshes cached icons.
- Retains v1.3.0's soundbar-reported ARC volume readings, soundbar and Apple TV remote support, smooth volume bar, and 0/100 limit-press refreshes.

### Upgrading from v1.3.0 or earlier

1. Install/update **com.github.nikolay1243.earcvolume_1.4.0_all.ipk**. There is only one IPK in this release.
2. Open **eARC Volume Overlay** and select **Enable**.
3. After verifying the unified app, uninstall the old **eARC Volume Overlay Settings** companion (`com.github.nikolay1243.earcvolume.settings`). Do not select Disable in that old app before removing it, as it controls the same watcher.

New installations need only the single IPK. The Homebrew listing now contains one app. Disable the unified app before uninstalling it.

### Validation and compatibility

Tested on the maintainer's rooted TV: settings Enable/Back, preview returning to settings, volume display over YouTube, pointer and arrow/OK controls while visible, hard reboot with automatic watcher startup, and standby/wake.

Requires root and Homebrew Channel. Window switching uses private webOS APIs and needs validation on other firmware. ARC volume and device-dependent volume steps retain the compatibility limits described in v1.3.0; no intermediate volume values are invented.
