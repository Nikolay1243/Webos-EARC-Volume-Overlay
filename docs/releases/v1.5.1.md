## Fallback for TVs without CEC reports in the log

- If `/var/log/cecmessages` is missing, or two volume key presses get no soundbar report in it, the native watcher now reads webOS's stored copy of the soundbar's last reported volume (`getArcVolume`) instead of showing nothing. This keeps 1.5.x at least as compatible as 1.4.0.
- The fallback polls every 150 ms around volume activity and every second otherwise, and backs off to every 10 seconds when the soundbar reports nothing at all.
- The TV's master volume is never displayed. Its changes only trigger the overlay (and supply mute) while the fallback is active.
- A single soundbar report in the CEC log switches back to the fast path permanently, so TVs like the LG C3 behave exactly as in 1.5.0 and never poll.

### Upgrading

Install the 1.5.1 IPK over 1.5.0, open **eARC Volume Overlay** and select **Enable** to restart the watcher.
