## Accurate ARC/eARC volume and smoother updates

- Uses LG webOS's ARC volume API (`com.webos.service.eim/getArcVolume`) to read the soundbar/receiver's HDMI-CEC-reported level, instead of relying on the TV's master volume, which could drift during rapid changes.
- Reads the ARC getter directly because some firmware omits low-volume subscription notifications. This fixes the overlay sticking at a low number before jumping to a higher level.
- Volume changes from the soundbar's own remote now trigger the overlay on the tested setup; previously they did not. Apple TV remote volume changes now trigger it too. Device support depends on HDMI-CEC volume reporting.
- Adds a thin, smoothly animated volume bar. The number updates immediately to the reported value, and successive updates reuse the visible overlay without replaying its entrance animation.
- Pressing up at 100 or down at 0 shows the overlay again where the TV's CEC activity log is available.
- Reduces routine logging; startup and errors remain available, with verbose diagnostics enabled through `EARC_VOLUME_DEBUG=1`.

### About volume steps

ARC/eARC reporting does not necessarily expose every integer from 0–100. On the tested soundbar, low volumes change by one, while higher levels use larger jumps such as **23 → 27 → 31 → 34**. This is device-dependent, not a universal eARC limitation. The overlay displays the device's reported level rather than inventing intermediate values or counting remote presses.

### Installation and compatibility

Install/update **both IPKs**, open eARC Volume Overlay settings, and select **Enable** to restart the watcher. Requires a rooted LG webOS TV, Homebrew Channel, and a device reporting absolute volume over HDMI-CEC.

Reboot, standby/wake, mute, and switching between TV speakers and ARC were tested on the maintainer's setup. Other TVs and soundbars, including the original reporter's Samsung HW-M550, still need broader validation. Firmware without the ARC getter falls back to master volume, which may drift; limit-press detection depends on `/var/log/cecmessages`.
